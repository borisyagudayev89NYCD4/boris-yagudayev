PUBLIC STATEMENTS

Positions

A reference page documenting positions Boris Yagudayev has publicly described. These entries record his views without independently endorsing or evaluating them.

Political identity

Yagudayev has publicly used both Democratic and communist terminology to describe his political identity and has also associated his politics with democratic socialism.

Campaign finance

He has described his congressional effort as a money-free campaign experiment, rejecting conventional fundraising and emphasizing communication rather than financial resources.

Cannabis policy

He has publicly supported cannabis legalization and liberalization of federal cannabis policy.

Drug policy & social assistance

He has advocated treating drug-related social problems with greater emphasis on assistance, inclusion and underlying social conditions rather than primarily through enforcement.

Antifascism

Antifascist themes have appeared in his public political activity. A 2016 published report documented his use of a white rose referencing the German White Rose resistance movement at a political protest.

Online political communication

Yagudayev has described social media as a modern public square and has used online platforms as a principal venue for his political communication.
