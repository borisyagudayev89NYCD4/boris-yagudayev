About

Boris Yagudayev is a Long Island resident and Boston University alumnus. Publicly available professional profiles associate him with vocational and employment services supporting neurodivergent people, including work with The Nicholas Center.

Public records also show a Boris Yagudayev employed by the Nassau County Board of Elections in 2012–2013, including service as an Election Clerk.

Yagudayev has publicly participated in Democratic and democratic-socialist political spaces on Long Island and has presented himself publicly in connection with New York's 4th Congressional District.
