About

Boris Yagudayev is a Long Island resident and Boston University alumnus. Professionally, he supervises vocational programming for neurodivergent students.

Public records also document earlier service with the Nassau County Board of Elections in 2012–2013, including work as an Election Clerk.

Yagudayev has participated publicly in Democratic and democratic-socialist political spaces on Long Island and has presented himself publicly in connection with New York's 4th Congressional District.
